# Research and experiment records

Complete these records before interpreting benchmark results:

1. `research_question.md` — scope, question, hypotheses, and non-goals.
2. `paper_claims.md` — exact claims in the paper and their test status.
3. `literature_review.md` — verified citations and concise reading notes.
4. `model_assumptions.md` — simulator, sensing, communication, and energy assumptions.
5. `experiment_protocol.md` — frozen comparison and analysis procedure.
6. `results_template.md` — one record per run or experiment batch.
7. `decisions.md` — dated decisions and reasons, including changes in scope.
8. `upstream_contributions.md` — optional, independently tracked community work.
9. `publication_checklist.md` — evidence, artifact, venue, and outreach checks.

Do not replace unknowns with guesses. Mark them as unknown, then investigate and
record the evidence used to resolve them.
